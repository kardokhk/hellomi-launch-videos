Hellomi: launch videos

Built for an Upwork application: a flagship "Talk to us" video and two use-case videos for a fictional app, Hellomi, whose core feature is a live, speaking avatar. The app, the people and the avatar are invented and AI-generated; the app screens are drawn in code as stand-ins for real Figma files and screen recordings.

How they were made

  • Characters: one approved portrait per person, placed into each scene so they stay the same person.
  • Motion and voice: open-weight image-to-video (LTX-2.5) generating picture and speech together, several takes per shot.
  • Checks: speech recognition compared every dialogue take with the script (73 of 79 said their line word for word), takes were ranked for camera drift, and the best were reviewed by eye.
  • Edit: phone frame, app screens, kinetic type, captions and sound in a script-based pipeline; loudness set to -14 LUFS. Text stays inside the TikTok, Reels and Shorts safe areas.
